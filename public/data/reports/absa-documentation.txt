ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS OF CUSTOMER REVIEWS

1. INTRODUCTION

Customer reviews contain valuable information about how customers perceive different aspects of a product or service. Traditional sentiment analysis generally assigns one overall sentiment to an entire review, but this can hide important differences within the same review.

For example, a customer may describe the food positively while expressing dissatisfaction with the staff or service. Treating the entire review as simply positive or negative would therefore lose important information.

This project develops an Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) system to identify specific aspects mentioned in customer reviews and determine the sentiment expressed toward each aspect.

The project focuses on customer reviews of a restaurant business and examines aspects such as food, staff, and service. This provides more detailed information about customer experiences than conventional document-level sentiment analysis.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Traditional sentiment analysis determines the overall sentiment of a customer review but does not necessarily identify what the customer is expressing that sentiment about.

For example:

"The food was excellent, but the service was terrible."

An overall sentiment classifier may identify the review as negative or positive depending on its implementation. However, the more useful business information is that:

* Food → Positive
* Service → Negative

The objective of this project was therefore to develop an approach capable of extracting specific aspects from customer reviews and associating each aspect with its corresponding sentiment.

3. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The main objectives of the project were to:

* Analyze customer reviews using Natural Language Processing techniques.
* Identify important aspects mentioned in customer reviews.
* Determine the sentiment associated with individual aspects.
* Move beyond overall review-level sentiment analysis.
* Generate structured insights that can be used to understand customer experiences.
* Present the resulting insights through a dashboard.

4. DATA COLLECTION

Customer reviews were collected for analysis and subsequently processed to create a dataset suitable for aspect-based sentiment analysis.

The project focuses on restaurant customer feedback, allowing the analysis to examine specific parts of the customer experience rather than treating every review as a single sentiment.

The repository contains the main analysis notebook, `Chicken_Republic_ABSA.ipynb`, as well as a Power BI dashboard file used to present the resulting analysis.

5. DATA PREPROCESSING

Before performing aspect and sentiment analysis, the review data was prepared for Natural Language Processing.

The preprocessing stage was used to clean and standardize the textual data so that subsequent NLP techniques could operate on more consistent input.

The processing included preparing customer-review text for aspect extraction and sentiment analysis and organizing the resulting information into a structured form for subsequent analysis and visualization.

6. ASPECT-BASED SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis consists of two important tasks:

1. Identifying the aspect being discussed.
2. Determining the sentiment associated with that aspect.

For example, a review such as:

"The food was great but the staff were rude."

can be transformed into structured information:

| Aspect | Sentiment |
| ------ | --------- |
| Food   | Positive  |
| Staff  | Negative  |

This allows the analysis to distinguish between different components of the customer experience within the same review.

7. ASPECT EXTRACTION

The project identifies specific aspects of the restaurant experience mentioned in customer reviews.

Examples of aspects investigated include:

* Food
* Staff
* Service

Aspect extraction makes it possible to determine which parts of the customer experience are being discussed most frequently and what customers are saying about those areas.

Instead of simply counting positive and negative reviews, the analysis can therefore examine sentiment at the level of individual business attributes.

8. SENTIMENT ANALYSIS

After identifying the relevant aspects, sentiment analysis was applied to determine the polarity associated with each aspect.

The sentiment categories used in the analysis distinguish between:

* Positive
* Negative
* Neutral

This produces an aspect-sentiment representation of customer feedback.

For example, a review containing both positive and negative opinions can contribute separately to the sentiment statistics of the aspects mentioned in that review.

10. BUSINESS INSIGHTS

The main advantage of the approach is that it provides a more detailed view of customer satisfaction.

Instead of simply reporting that customers are generally positive or negative, the analysis can answer questions such as:

* How do customers feel about the food?
* How do customers feel about staff interactions?
* Which aspects receive the most negative feedback?
* Which aspects receive the most positive feedback?
* What areas of the customer experience may require attention?

This type of analysis can help businesses identify specific operational areas that influence customer satisfaction.

11. DATA VISUALIZATION AND DASHBOARD

The dashboard provides a visual representation of the extracted aspect and sentiment information, allowing the results to be examined more easily than through raw textual data alone.

The Power BI project is included in the repository as:

The dashboard complements the NLP workflow by converting the extracted customer-feedback information into business-oriented visual insights.

13. PROJECT OUTPUT

The project produces structured aspect-level sentiment information from customer reviews.

Instead of producing only an overall sentiment label for each review, the system identifies the aspects being discussed and associates sentiment with those aspects.

The results can subsequently be analyzed through the accompanying Power BI dashboard.

14. LIMITATIONS

Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis can be challenging because customer reviews often contain informal language, ambiguous expressions, sarcasm, spelling errors, and multiple opinions within the same sentence.

A review may also discuss several aspects simultaneously, with different sentiments associated with each aspect.

Consequently, aspect extraction and sentiment classification can be affected by the complexity and context of natural language.

15. CONCLUSION

This project demonstrates the application of Natural Language Processing to customer-review analysis using Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis.

Rather than treating an entire review as a single sentiment, the approach identifies specific aspects of the customer experience and determines the sentiment associated with each aspect.

The resulting analysis provides more granular information about customer opinions and can help businesses understand which areas of their service generate positive or negative feedback.

The project combines NLP, sentiment analysis, data analysis, and business intelligence through the integration of Python-based analysis with a Power BI dashboard.
